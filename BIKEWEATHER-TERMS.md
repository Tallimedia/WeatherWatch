# Bike Weather — Terms of Service

_Effective 2026-10-05. Contact: iot@tallimedia.com_

Bike Weather ("the app") shows Finnish weather, a short rain nowcast and a rain radar
picture at your position on a Garmin Edge bike computer. It is a personal project,
provided free of charge and without any service agreement.

## Not a safety system

**Weather and storms are a real hazard when riding, so this is stated plainly.**

The app reports what measuring instruments and models were saying a few minutes ago,
for a position that may be kilometres from the nearest instrument, together with a short
forecast of rain.

- It is **general information only**. It is not a warning system and not a substitute for
  your own judgement or for official warnings.
- **Conditions change quickly and vary over short distances.** A radar picture is already
  minutes old when you see it, and the nowcast can miss a shower or a storm cell
  entirely. "No rain expected" is not a promise that none will fall.
- Lightning information shows strikes that have already happened. It cannot tell you
  whether the next one will be near you.
- Data can be delayed, wrong or missing, and the app depends on your phone's connection.
  When the connection is lost the app keeps showing the last reading and says how old it
  is — check that age before relying on what you see.
- Alerts (a tone or an on-screen message) can fail to appear. Do not rely on them.
- Always follow official warnings and the conditions you can actually see.

## Use of the app

Use it only in a way that is legal and safe where you are riding. Keep your eyes on the
road or trail: look at the screen only when it is safe to do so, and never interact with
it while moving through traffic or technical terrain.

on Garmin's apps and devices, and on a backend service run by one person. Any of these
may be unavailable, may change, or may stop. Coverage is Finland only.
