Design preview

A mockup of an unreleased app, drawn at half size — the real screen is 800 × 1280. It shows live data from the same backend the app uses, and is deliberately limited to what Android's car templates can actually render, checked against the shipped androidx.car.app 1.7.0: a row is an icon, a title and at most two secondary lines; a grid tile is an image, a title and one line; six items is the safe budget while moving; and a tab holds a single template, so rows and tiles only mix via the still-experimental SectionedItemTemplate.

What the data actually contains

Every field the three sources hold for the selected place, live, so the question "what could this show" has an answer you can read rather than guess at. Generated from the responses themselves — a hand-written list would be wrong within a month, and wrong here means ruling out a field that exists. shown is drawn in the mockup above; available is served and unused.
