Finland's own road and weather data, in the car.

Road surface temperature, ice risk, traffic warnings and weather for cars with Google built-in — on FMI and Fintraffic open data.

Screens
Features

Road surface

Surface temperature from the nearest road weather station, with the station named and its distance shown. Road condition — dry, moist, wet or worse — from Fintraffic's own sensors, plus an outlook for the hours ahead.

Ice risk

A frost and ice warning derived from the numbers that decide it: surface temperature against freezing, and against the dew point. Air temperature above zero does not mean the road is.

Warnings

Nearby road notices from Fintraffic — lane closures, roadworks, speed restrictions — sorted by distance, plus official weather warnings from the Finnish Meteorological Institute.

Weather

Current conditions and the forecast for your location, from FMI's own post-processed forecast. Forecast hours shown with at-a-glance icons.
