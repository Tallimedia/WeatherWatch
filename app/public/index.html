What the app shows

  • Current conditions and a 10-day forecast for your place, with the reporting station named and its distance shown.
  • Wind and gusts from the Finnish marine station you choose — Harmaja, Utö, Kalbådagrund and 20 others.
  • Wave height, period, direction and sea water temperature from FMI's wave buoys.
  • Your own wind, gust and wave limits, so the number turns red when it matters to you.
  • Finnish, Swedish and English.

Wave buoys are lifted out of the water roughly December–April, so wave readings are seasonal. Everything above is live on this page — it is the same data the watch receives.

Weather data: Finnish Meteorological Institute open data, licensed CC BY 4.0. FIWeatherWatch is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by FMI or Garmin. Times shown in Finnish local time.
