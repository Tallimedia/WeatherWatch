What the watch shows

Your limits

About the data

Wind is a 10-minute mean

The meteorological standard, and the same basis FMI's own marine warnings use. Gust is the highest gust in that window, not an average.

Every value is timestamped

Stations update at different rates — Harmaja republishes wind every minute, most stations every ten — so each reading shows when it was measured rather than assuming.

Wave buoys are seasonal

They are lifted out of the water roughly December to April. When none is reporting, the page says so and falls back to model output, clearly labelled.
