# FIWeatherWatch — Terms of Service

_Effective 2026-09-21. Contact: iot@tallimedia.com_

FIWeatherWatch ("the app") is a personal, non-commercial project that shows
Finnish Meteorological Institute weather, marine and wave data on a Garmin
watch.

## Use of the app

The app is free to use and needs no account. It reads public open data and
displays it; it sends no commands and changes nothing outside your own watch.

## Not for navigation or safety

**Do not rely on the app for anything safety-critical**, including decisions
about going to sea.

Readings may be delayed, incomplete or unavailable. Weather stations report on
different schedules and sometimes stop reporting entirely; wave buoys are lifted
out of the water for the winter; and the nearest reporting buoy may be a long way
from where you are, which is why the app shows you its name and distance. When a
value is modelled rather than measured, the app says so — but a model is still a
model.

The app is not a navigational aid and is not a substitute for official marine
warnings, forecasts, or charts. For warnings that matter, consult the Finnish
Meteorological Institute directly.
