# Finnish RoadWeather — Terms of Service

_Effective 2026-09-21. Contact: iot@tallimedia.com_

Finnish RoadWeather ("the app") shows Finnish weather and road surface conditions on
cars with Google built-in. It is a personal project, provided free of charge and without
any service agreement.

## Not a safety system

**This matters more here than in most apps, so it is stated plainly.**

The app reports what measuring instruments were reporting a few minutes ago at the
nearest station, which may be kilometres from where you are, together with a forecast
and a derived indication of ice risk.

- It is **general information only**. It is not a warning system, not a road safety
  device, and not a substitute for your own judgement.
- **Conditions vary over short distances.** Bridges, shaded stretches and open ground
  freeze before the road a station stands on. A reading of "no ice risk" is not a promise
  that the road ahead is clear.
- Observations and forecasts can be delayed, wrong, or missing entirely. Stations fail,
  and forecasts are not always backed by a nearby station — the app says so when it
  knows.
- Always follow official warnings, road signs and the conditions you can actually see,
  and drive accordingly.

## Use of the app

Use it only in a way that is legal and safe where you are driving. Do not rely on it for
anything where being wrong would matter.

## Availability

The app depends on data published by the Finnish Meteorological Institute and Fintraffic
and on a backend service run by one person. Any of these may be unavailable, may change,
or may stop. Coverage is Finland only.
